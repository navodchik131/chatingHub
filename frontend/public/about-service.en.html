Character

Not a folder of images, but a persona

A character is your full virtual model: look, personality, backstory, and the channels she is tied to. Everything else in the service links back to her.

  • Photos and look — references and a text description that flows into every generation
  • Persona — what AI uses to reply when the operator is asleep
  • EXIF — metadata and geo as if shot on her phone
  • History — what happened with the model and how many credits were spent

Studio · Video

The frame starts moving

Take a ready frame from the archive and describe motion in words or show reference video. Motion Control repeats movement from your clip; prompt mode creates its own.

  • First frame — from studio archive or your own file
  • Reference — MP4 up to 30 seconds
  • Quality — 720p, 1080p or 4K, 5 or 10 seconds
  • 40 credits per clip, download as MP4

Frames and clips stay in the archive for about four days — download what matters right away.

Connections

A channel connects in a minute

Create a bot in BotFather, paste the token — webhook sets itself up. Then bind a character and choose whether auto-replies are on.

  • Telegram — multiple bots, each with its own character and delay
  • Fanvue — OAuth account connection
  • WaveSpeed — generation engine; on Pro you can use your own key and skip credits
  • Tribute — your own account for donations and paid chats
  • Notifications — browser push for new messages and donations
